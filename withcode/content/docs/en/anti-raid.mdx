# Anti-raid and lockdown

A raid is a sudden influx of accounts, usually bots, flooding your channels. WithBot counts joins within a time window.

## Thresholds

Default: **10 joins within 10 seconds**. Lower it to 5 on small servers, raise it to 20–30 on large ones.

## Account age

Set a minimum account age, e.g. **7 days**. Fresh accounts during a raid receive the chosen action.

## Lockdown

Lockdown blocks `@everyone` from sending messages in every channel. Enable it:

- with one click in the dashboard (**Anti-raid** tab),
- with the `/lockdown` command.

To end it: `/unlock` or the dashboard button. The bot restores previous channel permissions.
