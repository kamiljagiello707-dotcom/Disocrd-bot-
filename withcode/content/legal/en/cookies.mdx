# Cookie Policy

## What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored in your browser. We use them only where necessary, and anything else only with your consent.

## Cookies we use

| Name                   | Category  | Purpose                                  | Lifetime        |
| ---------------------- | --------- | ---------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| `authjs.session-token` | essential | dashboard sign-in session                | 30 days         |
| `authjs.csrf-token`    | essential | sign-in form protection                  | session         |
| `NEXT_LOCALE`          | essential | remembering your language                | 1 year          |
| `wc_consent`           | essential | storing your consent choice              | 180 days        |
| analytics              | analytics | anonymous statistics (only with consent) | up to 13 months |

## Changing consent

You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" button in the footer. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.
