# Privacy Policy

The data controller is WithCode.pl. We process data in accordance with the GDPR.

## What we process

| Data                                | Purpose                                             | Legal basis               |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Discord account ID, name and avatar | dashboard sign-in                                   | Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR         |
| Server list (`guilds` scope)        | showing the servers you manage                      | Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR         |
| Server configuration and audit log  | operating the bot, auditing                         | Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR |
| Moderation cases (user ID, reason)  | punishment system run on behalf of the server admin | Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR         |
| Contact form (name, email, message) | replying to you                                     | Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR         |
| Hashed IP address                   | spam and abuse protection                           | Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR         |

## What we don't do

- We never ask for your password or your Discord email.
- We don't store message content unless a server admin enables deleted-message logs.
- We don't sell data or profile users.

## Retention

Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days. Moderation cases are kept while the bot is on the server and deleted 30 days after it is removed. Contact messages — up to 12 months.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to processing of your data, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Write to us via the [contact form](/en/contact).

## Recipients

Hosting and database providers (EEA), Discord Inc. (OAuth2 sign-in), optionally Sentry (error logs without personal data) and Cloudflare (Turnstile).
